# Google Play listing copy

**App name (30 max):** RepliPlate: Copycat Recipes

**Short description (80 max):**
Make restaurant favorites at home. AI plans your week and builds the grocery list.

**Full description:**
Love the burrito bowl but not the $14 price tag? RepliPlate shows you how to make restaurant favorites at home, and exactly how much you keep.

CLONE ANY DISH
• Search a menu item or snap a photo of the plate, and get a copycat recipe with step-by-step instructions.
• See restaurant price vs. home cost and calories side by side.
• Vegan, vegetarian, lighter, and high-protein swaps for every clone.

PLAN THE WEEK WITH AI
• Tell it how many days, who's eating, your diet, budget, and goals.
• Get a full week of copycat meals that fits, with total savings.
• Swap any meal with one tap.

ONE GROCERY LIST
• Send a clone or a whole week to your list. Matching ingredients are combined and grouped by aisle.
• Check items off as you shop.

SHARE WITH YOUR HOUSEHOLD
• Share a plan, a recipe, or your grocery list by text or any app. They open right in RepliPlate.

WORKS OFFLINE
• Your saved clones, plans, and list work with no signal, right in the kitchen.

No account. No ads. Your data stays on your phone.

RepliPlate isn't affiliated with any restaurant brand named in the app. Prices and nutrition are estimates.
