Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

The short version

RepliPlate has no accounts. Your saved clones, meal plans, grocery list, and settings are stored on your device and never uploaded to us.

What leaves your device

  • AI requests. When you ask RepliPlate to clone a dish or plan your week, the text you typed, your planner choices (days, household size, diet, budget, notes), and any photo you choose are sent to our server and passed to our AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the result. We don't store photos or requests after the response is returned, apart from short-lived, anonymized rate-limit counters.
  • Share links. When you share a clone or plan, its contents are packed into the link itself. Anyone with the link can open it.
  • Fonts. The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of a normal web request.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no tracking pixels, no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No location, contacts, or camera access unless you choose to take a photo for a clone.

Deleting your data

Because data lives on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing site data for repliplate.app deletes it completely. You can also remove individual saved items in the app.

Children

RepliPlate is not directed to children under 13.

AI-generated content

Recipes, prices, and calorie numbers are estimates. Always check allergens and cooking temperatures. Use “Report this result” in the app to flag anything inaccurate or inappropriate.
